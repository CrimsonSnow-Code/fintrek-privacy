FinTrek Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

In short: FinTrek keeps everything you enter on your own phone. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your data. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Who we are

FinTrek is a personal spending tracker published by CrimsonSnow ("we", "us"). You can contact us at tommyzhanrong@gmail.com.

What the app stores

When you use FinTrek, the information you enter is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. This includes:

  • the expenses you record (amount, category, date and note);
  • custom categories you create;
  • your settings, such as currency, monthly budget, home page title and color theme.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect any personal information.
  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not send your data to our servers or to anyone else. The app does not need an internet connection to work.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Device backups

If you have Android's device backup turned on, Android may include FinTrek's data in your Google backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is managed by Google and your device settings, not by us. You can control it in your phone's system settings.

Your control and deleting your data

You can edit or delete any expense inside the app. To remove all of your data, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings (Settings > Apps > FinTrek > Storage > Clear data) or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy of your data, nothing is kept elsewhere.

Not financial advice

FinTrek is a record-keeping tool. Its totals, charts and budget figures are for your personal information only and are not financial, tax or investment advice.

Children

FinTrek is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no data, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, adding cloud sync), we will update this page and the date above before releasing that change.
